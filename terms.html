1. Terms of Service

Welcome to THE EGYPTIAN KING. By accessing our website and purchasing our products, you agree to abide by the following terms and conditions. All content, imagery, and designs on this site are the exclusive property of THE EGYPTIAN KING and may not be reproduced without written consent.

You agree to provide accurate and complete information when creating an account or placing an order. We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any orders that appear fraudulent or violate our policies.

2. Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. THE EGYPTIAN KING collects personal information (such as name, email, phone, and shipping address) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling orders, providing customer support, and improving our services.

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL) and secure third-party payment processors (PayPal, Google Pay) to protect your data. We do not sell or distribute your personal information to third-party marketers. By using our site, you consent to our data collection practices.

3. Refund & Return Policy

14-Day Return Window: We accept returns within 14 days of delivery, provided the items are unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached.

  • Defective Items: If your item arrives with a manufacturing defect, we will provide a full refund or replacement at our cost.
  • Custom Apparel: Due to the custom nature of our premium 500 GSM garments, we cannot accept returns for incorrect sizing. Please refer to our Size Guide before ordering.
  • Refund Processing: Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

4. Shipping Policy

Processing Time: Orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Because our apparel features custom calligraffiti printing, some items may require additional processing time.

Shipping Rates: Shipping costs are calculated based on the weight of your order and your location. Hoodies feature a flat shipping rate, which is shown at checkout.

International Shipping: We offer worldwide delivery. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or taxes applied to international orders upon arrival in their destination country.

For any questions regarding these policies, please contact us via our WhatsApp support or social media channels.
