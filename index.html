02 / MANIFESTO

"WE BRIDGED ANCIENT EGYPTIAN ICONOGRAPHY WITH MODERN STREETWEAR ARCHITECTURE."

THE EGYPTIAN KING was established to redefine local apparel production. Rather than following fast-fashion cycles, every drop is treated as an architectural study in fabric weight, silhouette, and symbol craftsmanship.

Our pieces feature clean lines, custom garment washes, and high-density cotton. Every release is produced in limited batches to guarantee meticulous attention to detail from the first stitch to the final garment bag.
